The problem

Your service requests live in a database. Your crews work them in the field.

To put work on the map today, most agencies bridge this gap one of three ways, and each one costs them.

Meanwhile the office and the field stay out of sync, paid for in time, budget, or flexibility.

The system of record (your 311, CRM, ERP, work-management or permitting platform) knows every job. ArcGIS knows every place. Between them sits a gap, and closing it is where agencies quietly lose time and money.

Approach 1 · Manual

Spreadsheets & re-keying

Crews enter the same job into 311, a spreadsheet and a map by hand. It's slow and error-prone, and updates get lost, duplicated, or logged days late.

Approach 2 · Commercial platform

A commercial field or integration platform

Expensive, and it brings its own fixed process, so you reshape your operation to fit the software instead of the other way around, and it still can't bend to the unique way each workflow runs.

Approach 3 · Custom build

A one-off in-house integration

Costly and slow to build, brittle when a connected system changes, and expensive to maintain, often living in one developer's head.

Each path trades away your time, your budget, or your flexibility. ORCSERVE takes a different one. It connects the systems you already run to ArcGIS, configured to your workflows instead of forcing you onto someone else's.

How it works

Three moves. One loop.

ORCSERVE reads the work you already have, publishes it to ArcGIS for the field, and writes validated field updates straight back, with loop prevention and a full audit trail.

01 / CONNECT

Query the source

Read new and changed work from CRM, ERP, 311, asset or permitting systems through approved APIs. You choose the records and fields.

02 / PUBLISH

Put it on ArcGIS

Work becomes live features for Field Maps, Dashboards, Experience Builder, Survey123, routing and analysis, on the platform you already own.

03 / SYNCHRONIZE

Write it back

Validated field updates (status, notes, photos, inspections) flow back to the system of record. It stays the single source of truth.

Not just status. ORCSERVE pulls the custom fields and the source images from your system and attaches them to the map feature, so a crew taps a point in Field Maps and sees the full picture: the request, the photo, the details, right where the work is.
