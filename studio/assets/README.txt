Hero image
==========
Drop your hero photo here as:  hero-worker.jpg

The Studio homepage hero reads this file (studio/assets/hero-worker.jpg).
Until it exists, the hero falls back to a solid dark background — no error.

Guidance:
- Subject (worker + phone) composed on the RIGHT; keep the LEFT darker/open
  so the headline stays legible over the scrim gradient.
- Landscape, ~2000px wide, good quality (no visible AI artifacts / gibberish text).
- NO identifiable agency branding — no "DC DPW", no city seals/logos, plain hi-vis
  vest (not an ORCSERVE-branded vest). Generic city street only.
- Make sure you have rights to use the image.
