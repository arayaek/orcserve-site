About ORCSERVE

Helping organizations get more value from the systems they already own.

ORCSERVE is an independently developed integration platform, built from public standards (Open311, the ArcGIS REST API) to connect enterprise systems of record with ArcGIS field operations.

Our belief

Enterprise systems and ArcGIS should work together.

Organizations have invested heavily in CRMs, ERPs, work- and asset-management systems, and ArcGIS. Yet field workflows are often disconnected, manual, expensive, or locked inside one-off custom software.

ORCSERVE closes that gap with a configurable integration platform that keeps the enterprise system as the system of record while unlocking ArcGIS for field execution, visibility, and analysis.

Your enterprise systems manage the business. ArcGIS manages the field. ORCSERVE connects the two.

Mission

Make enterprise-to-field integration configurable, reusable, secure, and accessible to more organizations.

Product philosophy

Leverage what customers already own. Configure where organizations differ. Reuse architecture wherever possible.

Initial focus

Municipal public works and other organizations with distributed work, operational data, and strong ArcGIS investments.
