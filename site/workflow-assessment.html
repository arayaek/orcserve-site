Workflow assessment

Understand the process before selecting the technology.

An independent engagement that documents how work moves from request to completion, identifies gaps, and defines a practical future-state roadmap.

1

Discovery

Interview business, GIS, IT, dispatch, supervisors, and field staff.

2

Current-state mapping

Document systems, handoffs, spreadsheets, delays, and duplication.

3

Gap analysis

Identify operational, data, integration, security, and reporting gaps.

4

Future-state roadmap

Recommend workflows, architecture, priorities, ROI, and phases.

Typical deliverables

A decision-ready view of the opportunity.

  • Current-state workflow diagrams
  • Systems and data inventory
  • Pain-point and risk assessment
  • Future-state operating model
  • Recommended ArcGIS workflows
  • Integration and deployment architecture
  • Phased implementation roadmap
  • Expected benefits and ROI framework

Assessment outcomes

Clear priorities
Reduced implementation risk

Stronger procurement decisions
