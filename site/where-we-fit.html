Where OrcServe fits

Built to work with the tools you already trust.

OrcServe doesn't replace the platforms your city has invested in — it connects them to ArcGIS field operations and adapts to how you actually work. Here's how we fit with the technologies you may already know.

Enterprise waste platforms

Routeware / Rubicon, AMCS

What they do well

Purpose-built to run an entire waste operation end to end — recurring collection routing, fleet and in-cab technology, service verification, billing, and compliance — proven at scale.

Where OrcServe fits

These are systems of record and operations platforms. OrcServe connects them — or your CRM / 311 — to ArcGIS Field Maps, adding flexible, per-request-type service-request workflows and two-way sync. It complements the platform; it doesn't replace it.

Route optimization

RouteSmart, WorkWave, OptimoRoute

What they do well

Sophisticated route optimization, especially for recurring, high-density collection. RouteSmart in particular is built on Esri ArcGIS and excels at large-scale routing.

Where OrcServe fits

OrcServe isn't a routing engine. For ad-hoc service-request work it surfaces ArcGIS's own routing, and it integrates cleanly with a dedicated optimizer when you run one.

Spatial integration / ETL

FME (Safe Software)

What they do well

The most powerful general-purpose toolkit for moving data in and out of ArcGIS — capable of building virtually any integration a GIS engineer can design.

Where OrcServe fits

OrcServe is the turnkey, supported solution for one specific workflow — service requests to Field Maps and back — with the operational logic already built. No build project, no dedicated GIS-engineering time. It coexists with FME.

Public-works asset & work management

Cityworks, OpenGov / Cartegraph

What they do well

Robust, GIS-centric management of assets and work orders. Cityworks is Esri-native and deeply capable for public-works operations.

Where OrcServe fits

OrcServe bridges external systems of record — 311, CRM, permitting — into your ArcGIS and field environment, complementing an asset or work-management platform rather than duplicating it.

311 & CRM (systems of record)

Salesforce, SeeClickFix, city-native 311

What they do well

Where citizen requests and customer data live and are managed — the authoritative system of record for the work.

Where OrcServe fits

OrcServe keeps these as the system of record and makes their work spatial, field-ready, and two-way in ArcGIS — no migration, no rip-and-replace.

Why we built it

We didn't build OrcServe in a vacuum.

Across this landscape of capable platforms, one gap remains: connecting the system a city already runs to ArcGIS field operations, adapted to how that city actually works — turnkey, two-way, and supported. That's the gap OrcServe fills.
