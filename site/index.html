The problem ORCSERVE solves

Three ways agencies manage field work today — and where each falls short

Most organizations rely on one of these approaches. Each can work; each carries real trade-offs.

Approach 01

Manual processes

Source system↓Excel↓Printed sheets↓Manual re-entry
  • Duplicate entry and avoidable errors
  • Delayed updates, poor visibility
  • Hard to monitor or scale
  • No integrated GIS workflow
Approach 02

Commercial field platforms

Source system↓Vendor platform↓Vendor app
  • High license and implementation cost
  • Limited fit for unique workflows
  • Vendor lock-in
  • Doesn't unlock the full ArcGIS ecosystem
Approach 03

Custom integration code

Source system↓Custom code↓ArcGIS
  • Expensive to build
  • Heavy maintenance burden
  • Knowledge trapped with a few developers
  • Every workflow a new project

Step 1 · Connect

Query work from the source system

ORCSERVE reads from CRM, ERP, asset, work-management, permitting, and custom systems through approved APIs or databases — you pick the records and fields that matter.

  • Select the records that matter
  • Map and standardize source fields
  • Validate before anything is published
Step 2 · Empower

Put the work on the ArcGIS map

Work becomes live ArcGIS features, so teams can run real field workflows across the platform they already own.

  • Field Maps, online and offline
  • Dashboards and live visibility
  • Experience Builder, Survey123, routing, analytics
Step 3 · Synchronize

Keep the system of record current

Field updates are validated and written back to the enterprise system — automatically, with loop prevention and an audit trail.

  • Status and completion dates
  • Notes, photos, inspections
  • Crew, asset, and custom fields

Built for your workflows

Your workflows aren't one-size-fits-all. Your software shouldn't be either.

No two cities run service requests the same way — and inside one city, a pothole, an illegal-dumping report, a downed sign, and a missed pickup each follow a different path.

All-in-one platforms bring their own fixed process and ask your operation to conform to it — the workflows that make your city run don't fit the box.

OrcServe adapts to the workflows you already run — per request type — and keeps your system of record in charge. The map fits your work.

  • Configurable per request type
  • Keeps your system of record — no rip-and-replace
  • Your process, made spatial and field-ready
