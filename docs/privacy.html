Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Orcserve collects through this website and how we use it. Orcserve is a product of Amrot, Inc., based in McLean, Virginia.

What we collect

This is a simple marketing website. We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Demo-request form. When you submit the form, we receive the details you enter — your name and email, and optionally your agency or organization, your system of record, and anything you write in the message field.
  • Basic server logs. Our hosting provider records standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use it to identify you.

How we use it

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your request, understand your needs, and set up a demo. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How the form is processed

The demo-request form is handled by a third-party form-to-email service, FormSubmit, which passes your submission to us by email. Your submission is subject to FormSubmit's own privacy practices in transit. Once we receive it, we store it in our business email and contact records.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set tracking cookies and does not run third-party analytics. We load web fonts from Google Fonts, which may process your IP address to serve the fonts; see Google's privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

We keep demo-request information only as long as needed to follow up with you and for ordinary business records. You can ask us to access or delete the information you submitted at any time — just reach out through the contact form, and we'll take care of it.

Changes to this policy

As Orcserve grows, we may update this policy. The "last updated" date above will always reflect the current version.

Questions about privacy? Use the contact form and note that it's a privacy request, and we'll get back to you.
