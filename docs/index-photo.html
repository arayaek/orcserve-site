Built for your workflows

Your workflows aren't one-size-fits-all. Your software shouldn't be either.

No two cities run service requests the same way — and inside one city, a pothole, an illegal-dumping report, a downed sign, and a missed pickup each follow a different path: different intake questions, crews, approvals, and timelines.

All-in-one platforms bring their own fixed process and ask your operation to conform to it. That's where the friction starts — the workflows that make your city run don't fit the box.

OrcServe adapts to the workflows you already run — per request type — and keeps your system of record in charge. The map fits your work.

How it works

Three moves. One loop.

01 / CONNECT

Query the source

Read new and changed work from CRM, ERP, asset, work-management, or permitting systems through approved APIs — you choose the records and fields.

02 / EMPOWER

Publish to ArcGIS

Work becomes live features for Field Maps, Dashboards, Experience Builder, Survey123, routing, and analysis — the platform you already own.

03 / SYNCHRONIZE

Write it back

Validated field updates — status, notes, photos, inspections — flow back to the system of record, with loop prevention and a full audit trail.
